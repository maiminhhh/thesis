Improvement over the unintegrated baseline as the number of shared orthologues falls, for the three inputs (+MAP +SAS). (A) Fitted improvement for each input against the number of shared one-to-one orthologues in the dataset, for the overall, bio-conservation and batch-correction scores, averaged over tissues and the six algorithms run on all three inputs (lines, 95% CI bands); points are the mean over algorithms for each tissue and dataset. (B) Difference in improvement between each pair of inputs (first minus second) against the number of shared orthologues: fitted difference averaged over tissues and algorithms (line, 95% CI band) and the mean over algorithms for each tissue and dataset (points); where the band is clear of zero, the two inputs differ at that number of shared orthologues. Tests of whether the inputs' slopes differ are given in Tables~\ref{tab:orthologue_anova} and~\ref{tab:orthologue_slopes}. Estimates are from a linear mixed model with input, number of shared orthologues and tissue as fixed effects and random effects for algorithm, algorithm by input, dataset and dataset by input, with Kenward-Roger degrees of freedom (Section~\ref{sec:stats_orthologues}).
