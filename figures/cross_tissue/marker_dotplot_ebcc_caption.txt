Expression of marker genes by EBCC label in each dataset: (A, B) colon, (C, D) bone marrow, (E, F) fetal liver and (G, H) breast cancer; human on the left, mouse on the right. Rows are EBCC labels; columns are marker genes on the gene panel, grouped by the cell type they mark (strips), with at most five markers per cell type (the first listed in the annotation; full lists in Table~\ref{tab:markers}). Dot size: percentage of cells of the label in which the gene is detected; colour: mean expression, scaled across the labels of the panel for each gene (clipped at $\pm 2.5$). A label without a strip has no marker on that panel; bone marrow mouse includes marker groups (Chondro/Osteo, Endothelial, Macrophage) used in annotation that are not EBCC labels.
