Marker AUROC of each annotator's labels in every dataset: (A, B) colon, (C, D) bone marrow, (E, F) fetal liver and (G, H) breast cancer; human on the left, mouse on the right. For each cell type (number of panel markers in brackets), the AUROC measures how well the mean expression of its marker genes separates the cells given that label from all other cells (0.5, dashed line: no better than chance; 1: perfect separation). Colour: annotation method (green, Seurat label transfer; purple, SingleR; black diamond, EBCC consensus); filled circles use the same-species reference and open circles the other species' reference. A missing point means the annotator never assigned that label. The bottom row (shaded) is each annotator's mean over the cell types it assigns. Marker lists are given in Table~\ref{tab:markers}.
