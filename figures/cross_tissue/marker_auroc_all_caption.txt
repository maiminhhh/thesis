Marker AUROC of each annotator's labels in every dataset: (A, B) colon, (C, D) bone marrow, (E, F) fetal liver and (G, H) breast cancer; human on the left, mouse on the right. For each cell type (number of panel markers in brackets), the AUROC measures how well the mean expression of its marker genes separates the cells given that label from all other cells (0.5: no better than chance; 1: perfect separation). Seurat and SingleR use the same-species reference; (cross) uses the other species' reference. A dash marks a cell type the annotator never assigned. The bottom row is each annotator's mean over the cell types it assigns. Marker lists are given in Table~\ref{tab:markers}.
