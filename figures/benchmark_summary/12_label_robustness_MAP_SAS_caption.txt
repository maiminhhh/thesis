Robustness of the overall ranking to the cell-type labels (+MAP +SAS score, 15 datasets: dissected and genes2--8 of bone marrow, fetal liver and breast cancer). (A) Weighted Kendall's $\tau_w$ between the rankings of the 23 integration methods by overall score computed with EBCC labels and with the best single annotator's labels (per species, highest mean marker AUROC), for each dataset; open points: the top-ranked method differs between the two labellings. Median $\tau_w$ = 0.94 (range 0.850--0.997); same top-ranked method in 13 of 15 datasets. Grey band and dashed line: interquartile range and median of $\tau_w$ between the overall rankings of datasets of the same tissue under EBCC labels (30 pairs; median 0.53). (B) Mean rank of each method over the 15 datasets under the two labellings ($\tau_w$ = 0.91); dashed line: equal rank. Colon is not included: EBCC is the best annotator in both species there, so the two labellings coincide. scANVI is trained on EBCC labels under both labellings.
