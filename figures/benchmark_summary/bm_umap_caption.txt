Bone marrow (dissected mouse data), 50,042 evaluation cells present in every output, all drawn in one random order. Top: species; bottom: cell type (EBCC labels). Axes zoomed to the central 98% of cells per panel.
Under each name: the method's bio-conservation, batch-correction and overall scores in bone marrow (benchmark definition with MAP and SAS; not improvements). ‡ graph-based: no embedding, so bio = mean of NMI, ARI, cLISI and batch = mean of iLISI, graph connectivity.
UMAP settings, identical for all five panels and for the unintegrated UMAPs of the other appendix figure: scanpy 1.11.5; embedding methods: sc.pp.neighbors(n_neighbors=15, metric=euclidean, random_state=0, all embedding dimensions); graph-based methods: their saved connectivities; sc.tl.umap(min_dist=0.5, spread=1.0, random_state=0, init_pos=random) for all. A random initial layout is used throughout because spectral initialisation fails on some of these graphs.
