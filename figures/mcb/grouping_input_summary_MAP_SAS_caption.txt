Gain from the alternative inputs over native input (+MAP +SAS), for the six algorithms run on all three inputs (Harmony, BBKNN, fastMNN, Seurat RPCA, Seurat CCA and Scanorama). Large points and bars: estimated gain in improvement over the unintegrated baseline (scVI latent space or UINMF factors minus native input) within each tissue and averaged over tissues (shaded row), with 95% confidence intervals, from the linear mixed model with input and tissue as fixed effects and random effects for algorithm and algorithm by input (Kenward--Roger degrees of freedom; Section~\ref{sec:stats_inputs}), fitted separately to each score. Faint symbols: gain of each single algorithm (same algorithm on the alternative input minus on native input); in the shaded row, its mean over the four tissues; triangles, fastMNN. Horizontal axes differ between scores.
